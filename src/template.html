Patterns

Drag to rotate. Scroll or pinch to zoom. Click a country to paint it, click again to clear it. Hold Ctrl (Cmd on Mac) and move over countries to paint many at once.

Subdivisions

Point at a country to load its subdivisions, then click to paint them. Regions and constituent parts (such as Scotland) paint all their units at once where the country has them. Some countries offer more than one layer (such as constituencies); a country is painted with one layer at a time. Painting a whole country again clears its subdivisions.
