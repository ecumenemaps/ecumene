Example maps

{count} maps made with the editor. Each one opens in the editor for you to change into your own, says how it was made, and names where its data comes from. Data read from another site is saved in the map with the date it was read, so these maps do not change by themselves. The one invented scenario says so, here and in its exports.

Ecumene is open source under the GNU AGPL. The code, the plans and a place to report mistakes are on GitHub.

No accounts and no tracking. This page loads nothing from other sites.
