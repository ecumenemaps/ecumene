Ecumene

Maps of the world that say where their borders come from.

Paint countries, outline regions, add arrows and labels on a globe you can turn, then export a globe or a flat map that names its sources.

Made to be checked

Every border comes from Natural Earth, a public-domain dataset made by volunteer cartographers with the support of the North American Cartographic Information Society.

Natural Earth draws borders where they are in practice. Disputed areas are not yet marked separately, and every exported file says so rather than leaving you to find out.

Each export can carry a small source line with the data, projection, version and date. SVG files also hold the full details inside, so anyone can see how a map was made.
